What is MITRE ATT&CK?

MITRE ATT&CK is a free, publicly maintained knowledge base that catalogues every known tactic and technique used by real-world hackers — from the first moment they target an organisation all the way to deploying ransomware. Security teams worldwide use it as a common language to describe, detect, and defend against attacks.

This platform teaches all 14 attack tactics through five interactive modes. No prior cybersecurity experience needed.
